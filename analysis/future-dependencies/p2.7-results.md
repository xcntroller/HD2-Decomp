## Commit arithmetic and exact draw order

`0068C300` first commits pending rank34 into record24 then resets34=-1; appends
pending medal38 to record+2C/+30 and resets38=-1. It adds eight FLOAT deltas
soldier10,14,18,1C,20,24,28,2C to record48,4C,50,54,58,60,64,68 respectively.
All seven destinations except48 clamp only ABOVE100; no lower clamp is present.
For record70==0 only, accumulate a survivor count, seven deltas14..2C and signed
score30. The FLOAT accumulator corresponding to10 remains zero. The loop's
REP STOSD clears nine DWORDs soldier10..30 after each consumption. At0068C3A2
EDI becomes soldier+10 and the0068C4E9 backedge returns to0068C313: decompiler
presentation as repeated local-array zeroing is misleading. Preserve the mutation.
Then reverse-remove squad entries whose HumanStore70!=0, destroying their owners.

If survivor count==0, no redistribution and no random draws. Otherwise seven
FLOAT means use reciprocal count, score mean uses signed integer division
truncating toward zero. Visit candidate HumanStore indices ascending, bounded by
count AND index<=29. Skip ONLY record70==1 (other nonzero values are not skipped).
Every processed candidate draws `rand()` NINE times in this order:

| Draw | Mutation |
| --- | --- |
|1|record38 = low DWORD of original __ftol conversion of existing signed score38 + F(draw)*integer score mean|
|2|record48 += F(draw)*zero accumulator (still consumes draw)|
|3..9|record4C,50,54,58,60,64,68 += F(draw)*respective seven means|

`F(r) = (r * FLOAT[0080FF64] * FLOAT[0080FAD4]) + DOUBLE[00815910]`;
constants are float1/32767 (`3.0518509447574615e-05`),
float0.8 (`0.800000011920929`) and double0.6. Preserve x87 intermediate evaluation,
the original __ftol truncation and FLOAT store points; do not replace with a
rounded float factor or change PRNG implementation/seed consumption. Seven upper
clamps occur after all nine draws. Existing squad survivors are not excluded from
candidate redistribution. No proof identifies these raw deltas as particular UI
skill names; use verified source mappings before naming them.
