## What counts as available

An existing partial needs review of its **entire original body and dependencies**,
not just the implemented happy path. Layout/ABI recovery, missing branches,
defined failure paths and original container/ownership work are tasks to do now;
they are not automatic reasons to defer to Phase2. A native guard difference
must be resolved or left explicit, never silently dropped to gain completion.
Original engine calls may remain genuine delegates in a complete client caller
once their full contracts are known; calling them does not reconstruct the engine.

Missing live server/UI acceptance alone does not block source-only reconstruction.
Use complete static evidence, native fixtures and strict i386 verification.
Conversely, acceptance of a screen does not complete its object/container ABI.
Never execute original game/DLL/server/Proton code or keep permanent new tests.

Only defer a body after documenting the specific unreconstructed receiver, data
contract or owner that prevents full implementation/verification. Record original
addresses and the work that would unblock it. A broad phrase such as “native ABI”
or “Phase2” is not a sufficient blocker. Pending research is not a demonstrated
blocker, and this queue does not label whole families blocked without that work.
